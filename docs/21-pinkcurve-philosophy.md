## Purpose

This document captures the enduring principles and philosophy that guide PinkCurve.

Unlike technical architecture or product requirements, these principles are intended to remain stable as the platform evolves. They provide guidance for product decisions, engineering, design, business strategy, artificial intelligence, trust, and community growth.

The philosophy described here answers a simple question:

> **What kind of company does PinkCurve aspire to become?**

---

## Discovery Should Be Alive

Discovery is not a static catalog.

Every visit to PinkCurve should reveal new opportunities, new stories, and new Offerings that matter to each Buyer.

A platform that never changes quickly becomes invisible.

A platform that continuously creates meaningful discovery becomes part of people's daily lives.

---

## Every Day Worth Opening

PinkCurve aspires to become a platform that Buyers naturally open every day—not because they have a specific purchase in mind, but because they expect to discover something meaningful.

Discovery becomes a daily habit rather than a transaction-driven activity.
