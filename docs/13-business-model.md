## Overview

PinkCurve's business model aligns platform success with Buyer value and Seller success.

PinkCurve does not primarily sell advertising impressions, nor does it process the transactions between Buyers and Sellers.

Instead, PinkCurve creates economic value by helping worthwhile Offerings reach Buyers who may genuinely value them.

For Buyers, PinkCurve provides a free, visual, relevant, and trustworthy discovery experience.

For Sellers, PinkCurve provides measurable discovery value through capabilities such as qualified Buyer visits, brand recognition, Offering intelligence, creative tools, and discovery insights.

Transactions remain primarily between Buyers and Sellers on Seller destinations. PinkCurve focuses on the discovery that happens before those transactions.

The economic model is therefore built around a simple principle:

**PinkCurve should earn revenue when it creates meaningful value for Sellers without compromising the Buyer discovery experience.**

```text
Sellers / Organizations
        ↓
Provide Offerings
        ↓
PinkCurve Discovery
        ↓
Relevant Buyer Exposure
        ↓
Meaningful Discovery
        ↓
Qualified Visits / Brand Recognition / Insights
        ↓
Seller Value
        ↓
PinkCurve Revenue
```

At the same time:

```text
Buyers
   ↓
Free Access
   ↓
Relevant + Visual + Trusted Discovery
   ↓
Meaningful Interactions
   ↓
Better Discovery Signals
   ↓
Better PinkCurve
```

This alignment supports PinkCurve's mission:

> **Help people discover what matters.**

## Business Model Philosophy

PinkCurve should not become another advertising platform where the Seller with the largest advertising budget automatically receives the greatest visibility.

The business model must preserve the integrity of discovery.

PinkCurve should therefore distinguish between:

* Organic discovery
* Paid discovery programs
* Brand Recognition programs
* Seller platform services
* Premium intelligence and creative capabilities

Organic discovery should continue to prioritize factors such as:

* Buyer intent
* Relevance
* Offering quality
* Trust
* Context
* Location
* Buyer preferences
* Discovery diversity

Seller spending should not secretly override these principles.

Where Sellers pay for additional exposure or promotional capabilities, PinkCurve should clearly define the service and appropriately identify sponsored or paid discovery when necessary.

The objective is not to maximize advertising inventory.

The objective is to create a sustainable discovery ecosystem in which:

**Buyers receive value → Sellers receive value → PinkCurve receives value.**

# 1. Qualified Offering Visits

A **Qualified Offering Visit (QOV)** represents a meaningful Buyer visit delivered by PinkCurve to a Seller destination.

Conceptually:

```text
PinkCurve Discovery
       ↓
Buyer Interest
       ↓
Click to Seller
       ↓
Traffic Validation
       ↓
Qualification
       ↓
Qualified Offering Visit
```

A QOV should not simply mean that a link was clicked.

Qualification may consider factors such as:

* Valid Buyer or session
* Not identified as bot traffic
* Not identified as click fraud
* Appropriate deduplication
* Minimum qualification criteria
* Other integrity requirements

QOV definitions should be understandable to Sellers.

---

## QOV Pricing

A possible model is:

```text
QOV Revenue =
Qualified Offering Visits
×
Applicable QOV Rate
```

Pricing may eventually vary according to factors such as:

* Offering category
* Market value
* Geographic market
* Seller plan
* Volume
* Qualification level

However, PinkCurve should not establish permanent QOV pricing before market validation.

Previous example rates should therefore be treated only as early hypotheses rather than business-model commitments.

Pricing should be validated against:

* Seller willingness to pay
* Alternative customer-acquisition costs
* Competitor pricing
* PinkCurve delivery costs
* Seller Return on Discovery
* Discovery quality
* Category economics

# Return on Discovery

PinkCurve uses the concept of **Return on Discovery (ROD)** to help Sellers understand platform value.

A conceptual model is:

```text
ROD =
Estimated Value Created Through PinkCurve
÷
Seller PinkCurve Cost
```

For QOV-based activity, a Seller may estimate:

```text
Estimated Value =
QOV
×
Seller Conversion Rate
×
Average Order Value
```

PinkCurve may provide QOV and discovery information while Sellers provide private business information such as conversion rate or transaction value.

PinkCurve does not need to process Seller transactions in order to help estimate discovery value.

ROD should remain an analytical tool rather than an assertion that PinkCurve caused every resulting transaction.

# Pricing Principles

## 1. Value Alignment

PinkCurve should earn revenue when it creates meaningful Seller value.

---

## 2. Buyer Experience Comes First

Monetization should not destroy discovery quality.

Seller spending should not turn PinkCurve into an overwhelming advertising feed.

---

## 3. Accessibility

PinkCurve should support participation by smaller Sellers as well as larger organizations.

Principles include:

* Low entry barriers
* Seller-controlled budgets
* Transparent pricing
* No hidden fees
* Ability to start small

---

## 4. Fairness

Pricing and discovery should be understandable and consistently applied.

PinkCurve should avoid arbitrary advantages based solely on Seller size or spending.

---

## 5. Transparency

Sellers should understand:

* What they are buying
* How charges are calculated
* What qualifies as a billable event
* How campaigns consume budgets
* What results were delivered

---

## 6. Sustainability

Pricing must ultimately support the real costs of operating PinkCurve.

A discovery system that cannot financially sustain trust, infrastructure, AI, support, and development cannot fulfill its mission over the long term.

# Billing Integrity

Billing must be based on trusted events.

For example:

```text
Raw Click
    ↓
Validation
    ↓
Bot Detection
    ↓
Fraud Detection
    ↓
Deduplication
    ↓
QOV Qualification
    ↓
Billable Event
```

PinkCurve should not knowingly charge Sellers for:

* Bot traffic
* Artificial clicks
* Duplicate activity
* PinkCurve test traffic
* Known fraudulent activity
* Invalid events

Billing systems should support:

* Auditability
* Event traceability
* Qualification rules
* Seller explanations
* Dispute handling
* Corrections where appropriate

Billing integrity is part of Seller trust.

# MVP Business Model

PinkCurve should not attempt to launch every possible revenue mechanism at once.

The MVP should focus on validating the fundamental economic hypothesis:

> **Can PinkCurve create discovery that Buyers value enough to use and Sellers value enough to pay for?**

The initial business model should therefore remain simple.

A possible MVP sequence is:

```text
Quality Sellers
      ↓
Verified Offerings
      ↓
Buyer Discovery
      ↓
Meaningful Interaction
      ↓
Qualified Seller Traffic
      ↓
Measure Seller Value
      ↓
Validate Willingness to Pay
```

Early monetization may emphasize:

* Simple Seller participation
* Limited Creative Studio capabilities
* QOV measurement
* Basic Seller analytics
* Controlled pilot pricing

More complex monetization can follow after validation:

* Brand Recognition campaigns
* Subscription tiers
* Advanced Seller Intelligence
* Enterprise services
* Premium creative
* API access

The purpose of the MVP is not to maximize revenue.

The purpose is to validate that the PinkCurve value exchange works.

# Business Philosophy

PinkCurve succeeds when Buyers discover Offerings that matter and Sellers receive meaningful opportunities to connect with those Buyers.

The platform does not optimize for impressions, clicks, advertising spend, or transactions alone.

It optimizes for meaningful discovery.

A healthy PinkCurve ecosystem should create a reinforcing relationship:

```text
Buyer Value
     ↓
Seller Value
     ↓
PinkCurve Value
     ↓
Better Platform
     ↓
Greater Buyer Value
```

PinkCurve's long-term business success should therefore come from making discovery better—not from making discovery more intrusive.
