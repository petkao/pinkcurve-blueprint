## Overview

PinkCurve is an AI-powered product discovery platform that helps sellers connect with qualified buyers. Unlike traditional advertising or marketplace platforms, PinkCurve focuses on the discovery phase of the buyer journey—helping buyers find products they want before they're ready to purchase.

---

## The Problem

**For Sellers:**
- Difficulty reaching qualified buyers who are genuinely interested in their products
- High cost of customer acquisition through traditional advertising
- Limited insights into why buyers engage (or don't engage) with their products
- Challenge of creating compelling product content at scale

**For Buyers:**
- Overwhelming product choices make discovery exhausting
- Advertising-driven recommendations feel irrelevant or intrusive
- Hard to find products that match genuine needs and preferences
- Limited trust in product claims and reviews

---

## The Solution

PinkCurve creates a discovery ecosystem where:

1. **Sellers** capture rich product knowledge and generate compelling creative content
2. **Buyers** discover relevant products through personalized, AI-powered matching
3. **Learning** continuously improves discovery based on buyer interactions
4. **Intelligence** flows back to sellers to improve their products and positioning

### Platform Flow

```
Seller → Product Knowledge → Creative Studio → Discovery Engine
                                                      ↓
                                              Buyer Interaction
                                                      ↓
                                            Discovery Analytics
                                                      ↓
                                             Learning Engine
                                                      ↓
                                           Seller Intelligence
                                                      ↓
                              improved Product Knowledge, creative, and discovery
```

---

## Key Components

### Product Knowledge
The foundation of effective discovery. Rich, structured data about products—features, benefits, audiences, differentiators—that enables intelligent matching and compelling storytelling.

### Creative Studio
AI-powered content generation that transforms product knowledge into engaging creative assets: scripts, storyboards, and video content that communicates product value effectively.

### Discovery Engine
Intelligent matching that connects buyers with relevant products based on intent signals, preferences, and context—not just ad spend.

### Learning Engine
Continuous improvement through analysis of buyer interactions. Every engagement (and non-engagement) generates signals that improve future discovery.

### Seller Intelligence
Actionable insights that help sellers understand their market position, buyer preferences, and opportunities for improvement.

---

## Business Model

PinkCurve generates revenue by providing value to sellers:

- **Qualified Product Visits (QPV):** Sellers pay for genuine buyer engagement, not impressions
- **Premium Features:** Advanced analytics, creative tools, and intelligence features
- **Enterprise Plans:** Dedicated support and custom integrations for larger sellers

**Key principle:** Buyers never pay. Seller success is measured by qualified discovery, not ad placement.
